About the App

Engineering Setup Assistant is a practical field tool for engineers working with CCTV/IP camera, access control and intruder alarm systems.

The app brings commonly required setup, testing, calculation and fault-finding tools together in one application.

CCTV / IP Camera

Features include IP addressing and subnet information, network checks, IP planning, camera and recorder setup guidance, PoE and bandwidth calculations, storage and retention calculations, camera view calculations, fault finding and handover checks.

Access Systems

Features include network controller setup, door and lock checks, reader guidance, access power calculations, door timing checks, wiring guidance, fault finding and final handover checks.

Intruder Systems

Features include control panel and IP communicator setup guidance, zone and input testing, power and battery checks, battery standby calculations, signalling checks and test records, and final return-to-service checks.

Support

If you experience a problem with the app, find information that requires correction, or have a suggestion for a future improvement, please contact the developer.

When reporting a problem, please include the app version and, where possible, the system type, manufacturer and screen concerned.

Important Information

Engineering Setup Assistant is an independent engineering aid. Manufacturer names are used only to identify compatible equipment and provide relevant engineering context.

The app is not affiliated with, sponsored by, approved by or endorsed by the manufacturers referenced within it.

This application does not replace appropriate technical training, manufacturer documentation, site procedures, risk assessments, or applicable standards and regulations.

Engineers remain responsible for confirming system-specific requirements and ensuring that work is carried out safely and in accordance with the requirements applicable to the equipment and installation being worked on.
