Support & Privacy Information

About the App

Test Meters & Info Guide is a practical reference application designed to assist fire and security engineers with the use of common test equipment and engineering procedures.

The app contains step-by-step guides, diagrams and practical information covering subjects including electrical testing, battery testing, current measurement, light-level testing, sound-level testing, cable termination and other field engineering procedures.

Support

If you experience a problem with the app, find information that requires correction, or have a suggestion for a future improvement, please contact the developer.

When reporting a problem, please include the app version and, where possible, a description of the guide or screen concerned.

Important Information

This application is intended as a practical engineering reference. It does not replace appropriate technical training, manufacturer instructions, site procedures, risk assessments, or applicable standards and regulations.

Engineers remain responsible for ensuring that work is carried out safely and in accordance with the requirements applicable to the equipment and installation being worked on.

Privacy Policy

Test Meters & Info Guide does not require users to create an account.

The app does not intentionally collect, store or transmit personal information to the developer.

Information entered into interactive guides is used only to provide the relevant function or calculation within the app.

Third-Party Services

The app is designed primarily as an on-device engineering reference. No personal information is sold by the developer.

Updates

Technical information and guides may be updated as equipment, engineering practices and applicable requirements change.
