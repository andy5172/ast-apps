Engineering Setup Assistant

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Overview

Engineering Setup Assistant is a practical engineering application for CCTV/IP camera, access control and intruder alarm systems.

The developer respects the privacy of users and has designed the app to operate without requiring users to create an account.

Personal Information

Engineering Setup Assistant does not require users to provide personal information to the developer in order to use the app.

The app is not designed to collect passwords, security-system credentials or other authentication information. Users should not enter passwords or other sensitive credentials into engineering notes or other free-text fields.

Engineering Information

Information entered into the app for engineering calculations, setup, testing, fault finding and related functions is used to provide the requested function within the app.

Accounts

No developer account or registration is required to use Engineering Setup Assistant.

Advertising and Tracking

The developer does not use the app to serve third-party advertising or to track users for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

The application is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies when users download, purchase, update or otherwise interact with the App Store.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Engineering Setup Assistant changes or if additional privacy information becomes necessary.

Support

Support information for Engineering Setup Assistant is available from the application's dedicated support page.
